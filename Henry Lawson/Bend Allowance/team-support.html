Bend Allowance Support

Developer: Henry Lawson
Contact: henry.lawson82@sina.com

Bend Allowance names the bend whose developed length is longer than the blank. Changing K does not rewrite the radius. The last bend stays. A file note does not change the flat.

The app works offline. It does not ask you to sign in and it has no purchases.

To dismiss the keyboard on the bend name, tap Done above the keyboard, or tap the empty page.

Write to henry.lawson82@sina.com if a bend will not stay or the call does not change.
