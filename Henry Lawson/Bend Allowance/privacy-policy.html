Privacy Policy

App: Bend Allowance
Developer: Henry Lawson
Contact: henry.lawson82@sina.com
Effective: November 19, 2026

Bend Allowance is an offline iOS tool for a sheet-metal bend. It names the bend whose developed length is longer than the blank. An angle outside 5 to 165 degrees is not stored. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Bend names, angle, radius, thickness, K, legs, and blank stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload those notes to our servers.

Files. A file is read only when you tap Read a note. The note stays on the device and does not change the bend.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, the call you choose leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
