App: Anchor Stub
Developer: Carl Walsh
Contact: carl.walsh41@163.com
Effective: November 19, 2026

Anchor Stub is an offline iOS tool for an anchor bolt. It names the anchor whose projection is shorter than two diameters. An embedment inside four diameters is not stored. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Anchor names, diameter, stick, and embedment stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload those notes to our servers.

Files. A file is read only when you tap Import a note. The note stays on the device and does not change the stub.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, the call you choose leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
