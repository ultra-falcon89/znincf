Anchor Stub Support

Developer: Carl Walsh
Contact: carl.walsh41@163.com

Anchor Stub names the anchor whose projection is shorter than two diameters. Changing the diameter does not rewrite the embedment. The last anchor stays. A file note does not change the stub.

The app works offline. It does not ask you to sign in and it has no purchases.

To dismiss the keyboard on the anchor name, tap Done above the keyboard, or tap the empty page.

Write to carl.walsh41@163.com if an anchor will not stay or the call does not change.
