Privacy Policy

App: Belt Wrap
Developer: Frank Crawford
Contact: frank.crawford13@163.com
Effective: November 19, 2026

Belt Wrap is an offline iOS tool for an open belt. It names the drive whose wrap is under 120 degrees. A pulley pair that does not fit is not stored. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Drive names, pulley diameters, and center distance stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload those notes to our servers.

Microphone. The microphone is used only when you tap Listen to the belt. The note stays on the device and does not change the pulley.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, the call you choose leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
