Belt Wrap Support

Developer: Frank Crawford
Contact: frank.crawford13@163.com

Belt Wrap names the drive whose wrap is under 120 degrees. A pulley pair that does not fit is not stored. Changing the center does not rewrite the small pulley. The last drive stays.

The app works offline. It does not ask you to sign in and it has no purchases.

To dismiss the keyboard on the drive name, tap Done above the keyboard, or tap the empty page.

Write to frank.crawford13@163.com if a drive will not stay or the call does not change.
