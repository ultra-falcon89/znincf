Hold Day Support

Developer: Carl Ward
Contact: carl.ward17@yahoo.com

Hold Day names the batch you open before its hold ends. Tap the day. A hold under 1 day is not stored. Changing the hold does not rewrite the opened day. The last batch stays.

The app works offline. It does not ask you to sign in and it has no purchases.

To dismiss the keyboard on the batch name, tap Done above the keyboard, or tap the empty page.

Write to carl.ward17@yahoo.com if a batch will not stay or the call does not change.
