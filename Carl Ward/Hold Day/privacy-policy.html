Privacy Policy

App: Hold Day
Developer: Carl Ward
Contact: carl.ward17@yahoo.com
Effective: November 19, 2026

Hold Day is an offline iOS tool for a shop hold. You tap the day you open a batch, and a hold under 1 day is not stored. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Batch names and opened days stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload those notes to our servers.

Camera. A photo is taken only when you tap Take a photo. The note stays on the device and is not used in the opened day.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, the batch list you choose leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
