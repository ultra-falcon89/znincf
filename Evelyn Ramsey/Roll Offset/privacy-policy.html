Effective: November 19, 2026

Roll Offset is an offline iOS tool for a pipefitter's rolling offset. It names the offset whose angle is more than 8 degrees from square. A travel longer than the stick is not stored. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Offset names, roll, rise, and stick length stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload those notes to our servers.

Location. Location is used only when you tap Mark this bay. The note stays on the device and does not change the offset. No map is loaded.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, the call you choose leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
