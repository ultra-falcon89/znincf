Roll Offset Support

Developer: Evelyn Ramsey
Contact: evelyn.ramsey39@gmail.com

Roll Offset names the run whose angle is more than 8 degrees from square. A travel longer than the stick is not stored. Changing the stick does not rewrite the roll. The last offset stays.

The app works offline. It does not ask you to sign in and it has no purchases.

To dismiss the keyboard on the offset name, tap Done above the keyboard, or tap the empty page.

Write to evelyn.ramsey39@gmail.com if an offset will not stay or the call does not change.
