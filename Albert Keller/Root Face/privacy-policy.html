Privacy Policy

App: Root Face
Developer: Albert Keller
Contact: albert.keller73@126.com
Effective: November 19, 2026

Root Face is an offline iOS tool for a weld prep. Land is wall minus bevel. A bevel that meets the wall is not stored. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Prep names, wall, and bevel stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload those notes to our servers.

Photos. A photo is chosen only when you tap Bring in a photo. The note stays on the device and does not change the land.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, the call you choose leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
