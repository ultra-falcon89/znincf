Root Face names the prep whose land is under 1 mm. Land is wall minus bevel. A bevel that meets the wall is not stored. Changing the wall does not rewrite the bevel. The last prep stays.

The app works offline. It does not ask you to sign in and it has no purchases.

To dismiss the keyboard on the prep name, tap Done above the keyboard, or tap the empty page.

Write to albert.keller73@126.com if a prep will not stay or the call does not change.
