Crown Cap Support

Developer: Albert Quinn
Contact: albert.quinn97@yahoo.com

Crown Cap names the bead that is larger than its hole. Changing the hole does not rewrite the diameter. The last bead stays. A photo does not change the bead.

The app works offline. It does not ask you to sign in and it has no purchases.

To dismiss the keyboard on the bead name, tap Done above the keyboard, or tap the empty page.

Write to albert.quinn97@yahoo.com if a bead will not stay or the call does not change.
