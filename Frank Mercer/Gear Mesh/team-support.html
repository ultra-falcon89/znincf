Gear Mesh Support

Developer: Frank Mercer
Contact: frank.mercer12@163.com

Gear Mesh names the pair whose backlash is under 0.15 mm. A center that overlaps the pitch circles is not stored. Changing the module does not rewrite the center. The last mesh stays.

The app works offline. It does not ask you to sign in and it has no purchases.

To dismiss the keyboard on the mesh name, tap Done above the keyboard, or tap the empty page.

Write to frank.mercer12@163.com if a mesh will not stay or the call does not change.
