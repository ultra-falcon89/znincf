Privacy Policy

App: Close Trace
Developer: Frank Harmon
Contact: frank.harmon24@126.com
Effective: November 19, 2026

Close Trace is an offline iOS tool for a nozzle seam. You trace the seam, and a stroke under 8 mm is not stored. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Seam names and strokes stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload those notes to our servers.

Files. A file is read only when you tap Bring in a note. The note stays on the device and is not used in the seam.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, the seam list you choose leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
