App: Bore Plate

Effective: November 19, 2026

Bore Plate is an offline iOS tool for orifice plates. You drag a bore, and the plate is named when it leaves 0.20 to 0.75 of the pipe. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Plate names, bores, and the pipe size stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload those notes to our servers.

Camera. The camera is used only when you tap Shoot the plate. The photo stays on the device and is not used in the bore.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, the plate list you choose leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
