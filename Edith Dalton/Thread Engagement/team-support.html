Thread Engagement Support

Developer: Edith Dalton
Contact: edith.dalton61@163.com

Thread Engagement names the stud whose engaged length is shorter than one diameter. A length deeper than the nut is not stored. Changing the diameter does not rewrite the engaged length. The last stud stays.

The app works offline. It does not ask you to sign in and it has no purchases.

To dismiss the keyboard on the stud name, tap Done above the keyboard, or tap the empty page.

Write to edith.dalton61@163.com if a stud will not stay or the call does not change.
