Privacy Policy

App: Thread Engagement
Developer: Edith Dalton
Contact: edith.dalton61@163.com
Effective: November 19, 2026

Thread Engagement is an offline iOS tool for a fitter's thread engagement. It names the stud whose engaged length is shorter than one diameter. A length deeper than the nut is not stored. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Stud names, diameter, engaged length, and nut height stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload those notes to our servers.

Camera. The camera is used only when you tap Take a photo. The photo stays on the device and does not change the thread. No photo library is read.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, the call you choose leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
