Pin Class Support

Developer: Carl Crawford
Contact: carl.crawford15@outlook.com

Pin Class names the pin that is larger than its hole. Changing the hole does not rewrite the diameter. The last pin stays. A photo does not change the pin.

The app works offline. It does not ask you to sign in and it has no purchases.

To dismiss the keyboard on the pin name, tap Done above the keyboard, or tap the empty page.

Write to carl.crawford15@outlook.com if a pin will not stay or the call does not change.
