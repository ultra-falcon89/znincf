App: Chain Sag

Effective: November 19, 2026

Chain Sag is an offline iOS tool for a chain span. It names the span whose sag over length is under 0.02. A sag under 1 mm is not stored. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Span names, sag, and length stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload those notes to our servers.

Location. Location is used only when you tap Mark this bay. The note stays on the device and does not change the sag.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, the call you choose leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
