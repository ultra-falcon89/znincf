Chain Sag Support

Developer: Irene Nolan
Contact: irene.nolan59@sina.com

Chain Sag names the span whose sag over length is under 0.02. A sag under 1 mm is not stored. Changing the length does not rewrite the sag. The last span stays.

The app works offline. It does not ask you to sign in and it has no purchases.

To dismiss the keyboard on the span name, tap Done above the keyboard, or tap the empty page.

Write to irene.nolan59@sina.com if a span will not stay or the call does not change.
